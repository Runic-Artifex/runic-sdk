- Disk image steps on macOS retry hdiutil's transient "Resource busy" and
  "Resource temporarily unavailable" failures for up to a minute: the volume
  checks' create, attach and detach, the packaging check's attach and detach
  (`eng/ci/hdiutil.sh`), and `dotnet runic package macos`'s disk image. A
  retried attach first detaches what the failed attempt left attached, and the
  last failure prints `hdiutil info` (#1018).
