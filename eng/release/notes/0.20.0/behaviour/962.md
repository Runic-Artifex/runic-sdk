- **The Bridge warnings `RUNICBRIDGE013`, `028`, `031`, `032`, `034`, `039` and `053` fail a build
  that treats warnings as errors** (#962). They now come from the analyzer as
  compiler warnings, so `TreatWarningsAsErrors` (or `WarningsAsErrors` naming
  them) applies to them, as the diagnostics catalog always described; the
  generator's warning did not fail such a build. Fix the declaration, or keep it
  a warning with `WarningsNotAsErrors` (or hide it with `NoWarn`).
- **Every Bridge warning fails a build that treats warnings as errors** (#962,
  D066.35). The warnings the generator still reports itself (`RUNICBRIDGE020`,
  and the analyzer's when analyzers are off) and those of
  the `Runic.Application.Views` build targets (`015`, `019`, `029`, `030`, `033`)
  are MSBuild warnings, which `TreatWarningsAsErrors` did not reach. With it they
  are now errors too, unless `WarningsNotAsErrors` or `NoWarn` names them.
