- **A trimmed or NativeAOT publish leaves Bridge blobs and uploads out when no Bridge uses them (#1193).** The generated
  codecs of a member that holds a `RunicBlob` or takes a `RunicUpload` now mark their assembly
  (`[assembly: AssemblyMetadata("Runic.Application.Views.Blobs", "true")]`). A trimmed or NativeAOT publish in which
  neither the application nor a referenced assembly is marked or names `RunicBlob` or `RunicUpload` in its metadata (a
  library whose Bridges a generator before 0.23 wrote) turns the new feature switch
  `Runic.Application.Views.Blobs` off: the producer no longer lists `blobs/1` in its hello reply or grants uploads, the
  CS-WebUI host starts no blob endpoint, and Runic Desktop serves no blob exchange. The trimmer then removes the
  Window's blob store, its upload folder and the CS-WebUI loopback blob server. On linux-x64 the scaling suite's
  CS-WebUI fixture is 365 KB smaller (7,431,440 instead of 7,796,672 bytes with 9 ViewModels), its Runic Desktop
  fixture 163 KB and its Translations fixture 353 KB. Such an application's pages get no upload grant, and
  `client.upload` rejects with `unavailable`, which nothing in the application could have read anyway. Builds,
  `dotnet run` and untrimmed publishes keep `blobs/1`; set `<RunicBridgeBlobs>true</RunicBridgeBlobs>` to keep it in
  every publish, for example when only reflection reaches a `RunicBlob`.
  See [Making the executable smaller](../../../docs/packaging.md#making-the-executable-smaller).
